Photos

    How to take the photos
    1. Walk half a turn around the part and take 4 photos: one every 45°, in that order.
    2. Same distance each time, phone level, part upright and fully in the picture with background around it.
    3. Scale: measure the circumference with a tape (best), or stand a long ruler upright next to the part, level with its axis, and tap its ends on each photo.
